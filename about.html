About Us

Meet the Team

Behind Roatan First Class Tours are Tannia and Ted, two locals with a deep-rooted love for our beautiful island. Driven by a passion to showcase Roatán's wonders, they are dedicated to ensuring every guest experiences the island just as we do—with warmth, pride, and local insight.

Our Roatán Roots

Roatan First Class Tours was born from a deep love for our island and a desire to share its hidden gems with visitors. We aren't just a tour company; we are local guides passionate about showing you the true soul of Roatán.

The First Class Promise

Our name is our commitment. When you book with us, you are guaranteed personalized attention, reliable scheduling, and a focus on safety. We keep our groups small to ensure you never feel like "just another number," but rather a guest experiencing the island in comfort and style.

Join us to create unforgettable memories in the heart of the Caribbean.
